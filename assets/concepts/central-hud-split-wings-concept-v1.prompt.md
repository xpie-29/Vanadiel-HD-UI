## Final prompt

Create a 16:9 bottom-center HUD concept over a split bright-outdoor and
dark-dungeon gameplay-style backdrop.

Use a centered round minimap, approximately 180–210 pixels in diameter, as the
visual anchor. Give it a thin Aged Brass circular bezel, dark navy inner ring,
restrained four-point architectural accents, a clear north marker, and
original abstract terrain lines. Do not add a separate compass.

Flank the minimap with modular split hotbar wings: two compact rows of six
square action slots on the left and two on the right. Each bank has a subtle
frame and endcap and must look complete if detached. Use dark navy interiors,
thin restrained brass borders, small corners, original abstract ability
pictograms, a few cooldown overlays, one selected slot, and small keybind
markers.

Place one wide low-profile experience/limit-point bar below the assembly as its
visual foundation. Use a thin brass edge, dark navy track, restrained
blue-violet fill, and the concept label `LIMIT POINTS 21,600 / 30,000`.

Show two small detachable recast-strip examples above the outer hotbar ends,
using `Haste 0:18` and `Provoke 0:27`. Show three small proposed on-demand
utility launch buttons labeled `LOOT`, `SYNTH`, and `POOL`. Recasts and utility
buttons are exploratory placements, not approved defaults.

Match the established Midnight Void, navy, Steel Blue, Aged Brass, Warm Brass,
sparse Bright Brass, and Warm Ivory visual family. Keep the construction
compact, mature, forged/enamelled, dimensional but not glossy, and readable
over both backdrop halves.

Every hotbar, minimap, recast strip, utility cluster, and experience bar must
read as an independently configurable module that also forms a cohesive group.
Do not create one opaque background plate behind the whole dock.

Exclude player and target frames, party frames, chat, equipment, inventory,
gil, market values, enemy lists, target-of-target, target casting, numeric
distance, prediction, recommendations, analytics, automation, portraits,
logos, watermarks, copied game icons, or third-party proprietary art.

Avoid a rectangular minimap, heavy filigree, bright gold around every slot,
oversized corners, glossy chrome, futuristic styling, malformed slot grids,
and dense unreadable text.
