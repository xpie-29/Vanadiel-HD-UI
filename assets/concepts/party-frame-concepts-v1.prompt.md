# Party and alliance frame concepts v1 prompt set

**Tool:** Built-in OpenAI image generation
**Use cases:** `ui-mockup`, `precise-object-edit`
**Date:** 2026-07-30
**Status:** Historical generation prompts; Proofs 1 and 2 rejected and Proof 3
approved

## Shared direction

Create portrait-free party and alliance frames in the established Vana'diel HD
UI navy-and-aged-brass visual family. HP is the dominant resource; MP and TP
use thinner subordinate tracks. Show name and main-job/subjob abbreviation.
Omit level numbers because their field-level native-source treatment is still
conditional.

Use a thin Bright Brass outline and restrained edge arrow for the current local
roster selection. Do not fill the selected row with gold. Status icons without
timers may appear only on the selected/targeted human member used for the
native-focus concept; do not populate every party or alliance cell.

Exclude portraits, passive member-target data, numeric distance, trust-level
comparison, target-of-target, target casting, enemy information, inventory,
gil, equipment, market data, prediction, recommendations, analytics,
automation, action buttons, logos, watermarks, copied game UI, and third-party
proprietary art.

## Stacked party proof

Create six equal individual frames in one vertical stack at mid-left. Use the
names Doover, Aelira, Kain, Valeria, Moogles, and Tareth. Show HP, MP, TP, and
job/subjob. Highlight Kain as the selected row and show up to three small
status icons on that row only. Keep small gaps between complete independent
frames and avoid one oversized background plate.

## Compact raid proof

Reflow the same six members and information into a precise 2-column × 3-row
grid suitable for healer/support placement nearer the screen center. Preserve
equal cells, readable text, the same resource hierarchy, and Kain's selection
and limited status treatment.

## Alliance proof

Extend the compact construction into three labeled columns: `PARTY A`,
`PARTY B`, and `PARTY C`. Each group contains exactly six equal portrait-free
cells for eighteen members total. Give B and C restrained cool-blue secondary
edge distinctions while preserving common Aged Brass structure and standard
resource colors. Highlight only Party A member Kain. Do not show focus-status
icons on alliance members outside the selected local-party row.
