## Final prompt

Asset type: 1920×1080 visual concept sheet for the Vana'diel HD UI combat HUD,
at the canonical 100 percent scale.

Create a polished, high-fidelity HUD concept showing one compact player frame
and one complementary target frame as a unified visual family, plus approved
player and target status treatments. This is a visual design proposal, not
production artwork.

Use a split-screen original fantasy MMORPG gameplay backdrop: bright outdoor
highland daylight on the left and a dark blue-stone dungeon on the right. Keep
the scene softly blurred so HUD readability can be judged. Do not copy a real
game screenshot or identifiable copyrighted scene.

The portrait-free player frame sits in the lower-left/center combat area and
contains framed HP, MP, and TP bars. HP is about 10 percent taller than MP and
TP. Show the player name “Doover” above and the job line “DRK 51/WAR 25”. Do not
show numeric distance. Integrate the player casting treatment within or
immediately adjoining the frame, labeled “Fire III”, with cool-blue progress.
Place three small brass-and-enamel TP threshold pips at the lower right, two
illuminated and one inactive. Use subtle directional ornament only on the left.

The lower-right/center target frame uses compact complementary geometry. Show
the target name “Goblin Mercenary”, one HP bar only, no MP, no TP, and no
reserved empty resource slots. Include a small original heraldic check-status
emblem and restrained reverse-direction ornament. Beneath or adjacent to the
frame, place a small tray of observed player-initiated enemy-effect icons with
tiny “EST.” timer labels, clearly distinct from authoritative player timers.

Place a detached small player buff tray below the player frame and debuff tray
above it, with original abstract icon artwork and compact native-equivalent
countdown numerals. Do not copy game icons.

Use a polished high-resolution evolution of a classic fantasy MMORPG
interface: mature, atmospheric, compact, carefully forged aged brass, painted
enamel, dark stone, and subtle magical illumination. Keep it dimensional but
not glossy, with crisp thin one-pixel-like borders and minimal ornament.

Use Midnight Void `#070B13`, Abyss Navy `#0B1320`, Vana'diel Navy `#111E2E`,
Elevated Navy `#182A3D`, Steel Blue `#263D52`, Aged Brass `#9A7535`, Warm Brass
`#C49A4A`, sparse Bright Brass `#E1BD69`, Warm Ivory `#F1EAD8` text, HP Green
`#3F9B68`, MP Blue `#397FB3`, TP Teal `#32A5A0`, and Debuff Crimson `#B64B55`.
Main frames are 88–94 percent opaque.

Use crisp readable clean sans-serif typography in Warm Ivory with a subtle dark
outline. Do not use decorative fantasy type for values.

Compose a full 16:9 concept presentation. Show components large enough to
inspect while retaining an in-game HUD relationship. Include a narrow
unobtrusive palette swatch strip along the top edge with color chips only and
no prose.

Use original assets only, fixed coherent proportions, and clear safe padding.
Do not include target-of-target, target casting, numeric or continuous
distance, equipment, inventory, gil, enemy lists, analytics, predictions,
recommendations, automation, party-member target data, logos, watermarks, or
copyrighted interface icons.

Avoid oversized gold borders, filigree, thick scrollwork, glossy sci-fi
surfaces, generic neon MMO styling, clutter, irregular generated lettering,
generic circular pips, and portraits.
